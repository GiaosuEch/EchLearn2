# Product Packs

Product Packs compose platform capabilities and learning-domain concepts into a marketable learning or exam experience without changing Platform Core.

## Language

**Product Pack**:
A versioned bundle of CourseTracks, content, rubrics, policies, entitlement requirements, and experience composition for a defined audience and outcome.
_Avoid_: platform, feature flag

**General English Pack**:
The broad English-learning pack for everyday proficiency across core SkillAreas without an exam-specific scoring contract.
_Avoid_: free IELTS, default course

**Conversation Pack**:
A speaking-and-listening pack focused on communicative practice, fluency evidence, and interaction goals.
_Avoid_: Speaking test, chat mode

**Pronunciation Pack**:
A pack whose claims depend on separately validated acoustic capabilities and explicit measurement limitations.
_Avoid_: transcript score, accent grader

**Exam Track**:
A Product Pack or sub-pack that owns exam-specific task types, rubrics, values, disclosures, content-validation rules, and claims.
_Avoid_: platform assessment, generic test mode

**IELTS Academic Pack**:
The first premium Exam Track, owning IELTS Academic tasks, band-like estimates, track disclaimers, and test-generation policies.
_Avoid_: platform core, entire application

**Pack Entitlement**:
The Product Pack's declared access requirement evaluated by Platform Core's Entitlement policy.
_Avoid_: premium route check, paid boolean
