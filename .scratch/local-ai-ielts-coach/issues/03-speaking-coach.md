# Speaking Coach

Status: ready-for-agent  
Phase: 3  
Blocked by: 01, 02

## Outcome

Deliver local transcription and measurable-signal coaching without retaining raw audio or fabricating pronunciation assessment.

## Exit gates

- Raw audio is ephemeral by default and sync is consent-gated.
- Required transcript/audio disclosure is visible.
- Pronunciation is explicitly not assessed until a validated acoustic evaluator exists.
- Unit, integration, build, privacy, and browser flow checks pass.
