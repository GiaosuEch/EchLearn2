# Track Modules and Content Registry

Status: ready-for-agent
Phase: 5
Blocked by: 01, 03, 04

## Outcome

Register isolated Product Packs with namespaced content, rubrics, routes, capabilities, entitlement, consent purposes, benchmarks, and generic fingerprint orchestration.

## Exit gates

- A minimal test pack registers without Platform Core edits.
- Invalid/duplicate namespaces and incompatible manifests fail.
- Packs cannot bypass policy or read another pack's private state.
- Protected curriculum/data/audio/migration paths remain intact.
